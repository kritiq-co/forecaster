# Secret Mode — in-room card

One page. Verify live Steam numbers before you go in.

## Say this early
> "Your two questions — what to do with the live titles, and how not to get overwhelmed at launch — are the same question. Both are: which signals deserve a response? Everything else follows from that."

## Numbers (secondary sources — sanity check)
- **Escape the Backrooms** — ~88% lifetime (~46k reviews) · ~86% last 30d · peak ~48.9k (1.0, Oct '25) → ~5k now · 1.4 patch 27 Aug, hotfix 7 Sep · PS5/XSX May '26, Switch 2 Jul '26

- **SCUM** — ⚠️ not Secret Mode's (Gamepires → Jagex → **Splash Damage, Feb '26**). Use as comparable only. 73% lifetime vs **60% last 30d** · peak 45k → 5.5k
- **Sheffield studio** — opened Aug '26 · Dan Secker + Jacob Habgood · 3 → ~20 people
- **Star Wars: Galactic Racer** — Fuse Games (ex-Criterion) · **6 Oct 2026** · premium, not season-pass

## The seven questions that matter most
1. Walk me through the last content decision on ETB — who proposed it, what evidence, who could say no?
2. A week after a patch ships, how do you know it worked? What's the number, who looks at it?
3. What do players think they bought? (ETB vs Chained Together — answer separately.)
4. Who reads review *text*, not scores? How often?
5. What's the monetisation thesis for a $5 game with 10M owners — is it written down?
6. What does Sheffield get measured on in year one? *(throughput = your opening)*
7. For 6 Oct: what did you agree in advance you will **not** respond to in week one?

## Plus the one nobody's thought about
How does licensor approval change your response time on a community issue? An unlicensed game hotfixes comms in an hour. Have you pre-agreed an escalation path with Lucasfilm?

## Close with
**Offer 1 — Launch triage for Galactic Racer.** Days, not weeks. Signal tiers, named owners, escalation thresholds, and a "what we will not do in week one" list. *Removes decisions from the week you'll have least capacity to make them.*

Then: Offer 2 (Player Trust health check on the two live titles) → Offer 3 (standing operating rhythm).

**Sheffield answers "can we ship it?" — Kritiq answers "should we, and did it land?"**

## Do the whiteboard thing
Build the Galactic Racer triage tiers *with them, live, in the room*. They keep it. They've now experienced working with you instead of hearing about it.

## Don't
- Turn up as a tool/dashboard — you're competing with their own roadmap if you do
- Tell them their games are dying (they're at 88–90%; the pitch is *protect*, not *rescue*)
- Lean on RuneScape more than once
- Assert SCUM is theirs
