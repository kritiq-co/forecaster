# Proposal: Neil's v2, reviewed

Neil's own draft with corrections applied. Structure, framing and wording are his;
changes are confined to errors, consistency, and four missing commercial items.

## Must-fix, found in the draft

1. **"Secret Code is joining the premier league but is starting to get growing pains."**
   Secret Code is the new internal dev team, standing up now. The sentence means *Secret
   Mode*. Getting a prospective client's own entity names crossed, in a document about
   bringing them clarity, is the single most damaging line in the draft.
2. **"before mistakes are made, from load and from judgement."**
   As written this says their judgement is faulty. The meeting point was the opposite:
   mistakes coming from load *rather than* judgement. One word, and it flips from
   generous to insulting.
3. **Typos:** "What good could like" → "could look like"; "a coherent a workable
   timeline" → "a coherent, workable timeline"; "program" → "programme"; "Live Op" →
   "Live Ops".

## Added, because they were missing

4. **VAT.** £84,000 with no VAT position stated becomes an awkward 20% conversation at
   invoice one.
5. **Start date.** The term is six months from nowhere.
6. **A review point.** The draft has notice but no review. Notice is an exit; a review is
   the scheduled moment to rescope and reprice, which matters since the draft already
   says March will need more.
7. **One line on holding the pen and handing it over.** The draft dropped the ownership
   area entirely. Politically sensible, since naming the Marketing/Commercial gap could
   read as criticism of Dan and Alison. But it also removed the answer to the obvious
   question: *why will this work now when it hasn't before?* One sentence restores it
   without pointing at anyone.
